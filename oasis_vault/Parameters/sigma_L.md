**Value:** 2.22d — ABSENT from the term
**Defined in:** not referenced in `calculate_order_quantity`

`d^2 * sigma_L^2` is the larger of the two variance terms for any line that moves, and it is **not in the formula**. Lead time is as variable as it is long: 2.22d spread against a 2.29d mean.
