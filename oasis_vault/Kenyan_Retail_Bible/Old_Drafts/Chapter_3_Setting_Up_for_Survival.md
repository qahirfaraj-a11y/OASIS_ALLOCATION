# Chapter 3: Setting Up for Survival

*A practical guide to physical store setup, licensing, and supplier negotiations.*

## 🏗️ The Physical Architecture
A store is not just a building; it is a high-speed funnel. Designing for **Throughput** is the difference between a long queue (lost sales) and a seamless conversion.

### 1. The Power of Layout (Zoning)
*   **The Velocity Zone**: Place high-frequency nodes (Milk, Bread) at the back of the store to draw customers through the "Impulse Zones" (Snacks, Chocolates).
*   **The Hot Zone**: Items near the checkout (Carrier bags, Sweets) must have a 99% fill rate to ensure the "Last Touch" is successful.

### 2. Licensing and Logistics
The "Retail Engine" requires a legal chassis. 
*   **The Single Business Permit**: Understanding the county-level costs in Nairobi vs. other regions.
*   **Health & Safety**: Critical for Fresh Food departments to minimize the risk of "Regulatory Shutdown" nodes.

## 🤝 The Art of Negotiation
As a new retailer, your leverage is your **Neural Data**. 
*   **Vendor Compliance**: Use the neural graph to show vendors their "Blast Radius" in your store. If they have low fill rates (like Gold Crown), use that data to negotiate better credit terms or priority delivery.
*   **Consignment vs. Cash**: Map high-risk/new SKUs to consignment models to protect your working capital.

## 🧱 Conclusion of Part I
We have defined the formats, the economics, and the physical setup. We are now ready to move from the static "Architecture" to the dynamic "Ground Truth" of daily operations.
