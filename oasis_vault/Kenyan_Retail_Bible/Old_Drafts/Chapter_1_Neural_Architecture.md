# Chapter 1: The Neural Architecture of Kenyan Retail

## 🧠 The Concept of the "Neural SKU"
In the Kenyan landscape, a product is never an island. It is a node in a vast, interconnected web of price, proximity, and preference.

### 1. Substitution Clusters
Based on our census of **23,511 SKUs**, we have identified that the "Neural Graph" is densest in commodity departments:
*   **Milk & Bread**: High-velocity clusters where brand loyalty is secondary to availability.
*   **Neural Link**: A break in the `Brookside` node immediately activates the `Tuzo` and `Ilara` nodes.

### 2. Connectivity Metrics
*   **Substitution Density**: The average SKU in high-income urban areas (like Rhapta) is linked to **4.97 substitutes**.
*   **The Monopoly Gap**: specialty items (e.g., specific Wine vintages) often have **0.0 connectivity**, making them high-risk/high-reward nodes.

### 3. The Role of OASIS
The OASIS system acts as the "Central Nervous System," monitoring these links in real-time to ensure that no node failure (stockout) leads to a systemic shutdown of the store's revenue.
