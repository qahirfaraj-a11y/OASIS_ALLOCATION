OASIS Retail Intelligence Connector

Turn your Odoo stock movement into ordering, transfer, and supplier intelligence — without exporting a single spreadsheet.

This connector securely streams opt-in stock-movement telemetry — POS sales, goods receipts, and stock-on-hand snapshots — from your Odoo instance to the OASIS Cloud Hub. OASIS turns that live signal into smart replenishment, inter-store transfer recommendations, and a Retail Central Intelligence view your suppliers can subscribe to.

You stay in control

Nothing leaves your system until you switch it on and paste the store ingest token OASIS issues you. You choose what to stream: sales, receipts, on-hand — or any combination.

Zero dependencies, safe to run

Pushes over HTTPS using only Python's standard library — no extra pip packages on your server. Batched, idempotent, and resumable: a missed run simply catches up on the next schedule.

Setup in three fields

Settings → OASIS Connector → paste your Hub URL, Store Ingest Token, and Store Code, tick Enable, and press Sync now. A scheduled job keeps OASIS current every 30 minutes thereafter.

Decisions in Odoo, in Odoo's own documents

Installing the connector adds an OASIS app to Odoo's own home menu, right beside Inventory. It needs only Inventory — Point of Sale is optional, and transfers work without it. Under Transfers → Suggestions your team reviews what OASIS would move and why — days of cover at both ends, the value at risk, and which job the movement serves. Approving one creates a native draft internal transfer; nothing is reserved and no stock moves until your team confirms it in Inventory.

The analysis itself runs in OASIS, not inside Odoo. What lands here is the decision and the reasoning behind it, in a document your warehouse team already knows how to work.
