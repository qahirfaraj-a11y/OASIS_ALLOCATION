Retail inventory intelligence · for Odoo

Your stock already knows what to do. Somebody has to say why.

OASIS reads what every branch holds and sells, works out what should move between stores and what should be bought, and puts each decision in front of a human with the argument attached.

It runs on your own hardware, against your own Odoo. Nothing is ordered and nothing is moved until one of your people presses Approve.

Two decisions

Move what you already own, before buying more of it.

Most chains run these as one job and get both wrong. A store runs dry while the same line sits dead in a branch four kilometres away; meanwhile the buying system orders more of it. OASIS separates them, because they are answered from different evidence.

Module — OASIS Transfers

Stock you have, in the wrong branch

Two jobs, kept separate so you can tell which one drove a movement: plugging a gap before a store runs out, and clearing capital that is sitting still.

  • Days of cover at both ends, and what each store actually sells per day
  • A release cap, so a donor is never pushed below its own safety floor
  • Approve creates a draft internal transfer — one picking per route, not one per line
  • Perishables are surfaced but never queued automatically; transit costs shelf life

Module — OASIS Replenishment

Stock you need, from the right supplier

What to buy, for which branch, against the lead time that supplier actually keeps — measured from their delivery history, not the one on the contract.

  • What is already in transit is subtracted before anything is recommended
  • Supplier minimums are checked against the lines you actually approved
  • Approve creates a draft purchase order per supplier, per store
  • Each order is aimed at that store's own receipt operation, so goods land where the demand was measured
The reasoning is the product. The document is just its output.

OASIS has always been able to write a draft order straight into Odoo. That was never the hard part, and it was never worth much: a draft order says buy 48 and nothing else. The buyer either approves everything without reading it or refuses the lot, and neither is a decision.

So every line carries its argument — the position it answers, the horizon it covers, and what it costs to ignore. A person can disagree with it. That is the point: when your branch manager overrules a suggestion, they are telling you something the data did not, and OASIS records where and how often it happens.

It is a review queue, deliberately. Not an autopilot with a confirmation dialog.

Modules & licensing

Buy the part you need. They install and run independently.

A chain that only wants to stop cross-branch stockouts takes Transfers and nothing else. A chain that only wants better buying takes Replenishment. Install both and they cooperate — a line too small to buy becomes a line worth moving.

Deployment

On your hardware, beside your Odoo, and it works with the line down.

The analysis needs full visibility of cost price, item master and receipt dates. That data does not leave the premises, so neither does the engine that reads it.

01

Install the modules

From the Odoo Apps list, or by dropping them on the addons path. The base first; then whichever features you bought.

02

Point them at your OASIS instance

Settings → OASIS → Connection. One endpoint serves both queues. OASIS runs on-premises, on the same network as Odoo.

03

Read the queue before you trust it

Run it alongside your existing process for a fortnight and compare. Every suggestion states its own reasoning, so disagreement is specific rather than a general feeling that the numbers are off.

04

Then start approving

Approving is the only thing that writes to your inventory or your purchasing. Everything before it is analysis.

Who to contact, and what to expect.

Getting help

Email support@oasisretail.co.ke with your Odoo version, the module and version, and what you expected to happen.

Screenshots of the queue are useful. Never send us database credentials — we will not ask for them.

Response times

  • Queue is wrong or empty — one working day
  • Module will not install or upgrade — one working day
  • Anything that wrote to your inventory incorrectly — same working day
  • Feature requests — acknowledged within a week

Supported versions

Odoo 16.0, Community and Enterprise. The modules depend only on Community modules (stock, purchase), so Enterprise is not required.

Included with a current licence: fixes, and compatibility with supported Odoo point releases.

Privacy & data

What leaves your premises: by default, nothing.

The modules

OASIS Transfers and OASIS Replenishment make no outbound connection except to the OASIS instance you configure, on your own network. They send us nothing.

Telemetry, if you install it

OASIS Telemetry is opt-in and separately purchased. It streams only the stock movement categories you agree to, filtered before it leaves your system. Cost price, margin and supplier credit terms are excluded and cannot be enabled.

Personal data

The modules process inventory and supplier records, not customer data. No personal data is collected by us. Full policy: oasisretail.co.ke/privacy.
